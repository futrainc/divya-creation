About us

A passion that became a trusted name

Creative artisan and founder of Divya Creation, specializing in handcrafted candles, resin art, and bespoke gifting solutions.

What began as a passion has evolved into a trusted name for personalized, high-quality, and emotion-driven creations. With a keen eye for detail and a love for meaningful design, ideas are transformed into tangible keepsakes that celebrate life’s special moments.

Beyond product design, art and candle-making workshops for kids and adults inspire creativity and empower people to craft their own masterpieces. Every order is made-to-order — ensuring exclusivity and a personal touch.

Handcasting

Handcasting is the art of creating a detailed replica of an object, body part, or figure by making a mold and pouring a material — resin, plaster or clay — into it. It captures intricate detail with high precision, preserving the exact shape and texture.

“Handcasting turns memories into art, capturing every detail in a timeless keepsake.”
04

Texture art

Texture art creates depth and dimension using tactile materials — turning surfaces into visually and physically engaging masterpieces. Gold leaf, sculpted ridges and earthy palettes made for the walls you live with.

Lippan art

Lippan art is a traditional Indian craft from Gujarat where mud, clay and natural materials are used to create decorative wall designs with intricate patterns — often embellished with mirrors and textures.

Mosaic art

Piece by piece

Mosaic art is a form of visual art where small pieces of material — coloured glass, stone, tiles or ceramics — are arranged and fixed together to create patterns, images or designs. These small pieces, called tesserae, are carefully placed to form intricate pictures or decorative surfaces.

Sweep your pointer over the tiles to scatter them — they always find their way home.
